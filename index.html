请注意： 1. 您同意隐私政策仅代表您了解应用运行所必需个人信息；非必要信息会在使用对应功能时单独向您申请授权。 2. 设备权限不会默认开启；敏感权限会在使用对应功能时弹窗征得您同意；您可随时在系统设置关闭权限，拒绝权限不影响其他无关功能使用。 3. 如您暂不同意本政策，App 将停留在欢迎页，您仍可阅读完整政策。同意政策后，即使拒绝定位权限，也可手动搜索地点查看天气。

一、我们收集哪些信息

位置（可选）：首次使用定位功能时，经您授权后通过 iOS 定位服务获取经纬度与精度，用于确定所在城市、显示当地天气与区域预警。仅申请“使用 App 期间”的位置，不申请后台定位。授权后，App 在前台打开时可能自动刷新当前位置天气，您也可以手动点击定位按钮。坐标仅用于向气象服务商查询天气，不用于广告。您可以拒绝定位，改为手动添加城市。

通知（可选）：您允许后，App 在前台查询到新的气象预警时生成本地提醒。App 被完全关闭后，不会主动获取新的天气数据，因此无法提醒新发生的预警。

本地设置：关注城市列表、温度单位、通知开关、隐私政策同意状态保存在手机本地，不上传服务器。

长辈速览与语音：长辈速览开关和已提醒预警的摘要标识保存在本机，用于记住显示方式和避免重复提醒。语音功能使用 Apple 系统语音合成朗读天气文字，不使用麦克风、不录音，不向我方服务器上传播报文字或语音。

我们不保存位置轨迹，不结合位置信息做用户画像，也不将天气查询信息用于广告。

二、我们如何使用这些信息

  • 展示您所关注城市的实况、预报、空气质量、生活指数与气象预警；
  • 在前台查询到新的气象预警时生成本地提醒；
  • 显示长辈速览，并在您点击播报按钮时朗读天气文字；
  • 履行法律法规要求。

除上述目的外，我们不会将您的信息用于其他用途，除非另行征得您的同意。

三、共享与第三方服务

我们不出售您的个人信息，不将其提供给第三方用于其自身的广告营销。仅在法律要求、司法或行政机关依法要求，或为保护用户与他人安全所必需时，才会对外提供。为运行对应功能，本App 会调用以下系统服务和气象数据服务：

  • Apple 系统服务定位、地名解析、本地通知和语音合成（Core Location、CLGeocoder、UserNotifications、AVSpeechSynthesizer）
  • 气象数据服务商：为提供地点检索和天气查询服务，接收本次查询的地名或经纬度，以及完成连接所需的IP地址、请求时间等网络信息；本App 通过网络接口获取数据，未集成第三方气象SDK

上述服务依其自身隐私政策处理信息，Apple 系统服务适用 Apple 的隐私政策。气象服务商的数据传输、存储规则由服务商自身隐私政策约束，我方不做跨境或境内存储承诺。

四、保存与保护

保存地点、设置、天气缓存与预警提醒摘要保存在手机本地；查询时经纬度或地名仍会发送至前述服务。天气缓存约十分钟内用于减少重复请求，失败时可作为历史资料展示；超过二十四小时会标为过期，请联网刷新。预警提醒摘要最多保存二百五十六条并在七日后清理。清除天气缓存可删除磁盘中的天气缓存文件；删除保存地点可移除对应收藏记录。要清除全部本地应用数据，请在 iPhone 系统设置中选择“删除 App”，仅“卸载 App”可能仍保留文稿与数据。本App 数据存放于 iOS 沙盒，网络请求采用 HTTPS。若您开启了 iCloud 备份，备份由 Apple 依其条款管理。

五、您的权利

  1. 撤回定位权限：iPhone 系统设置关闭位置权限；
  2. 关闭通知：系统设置或 App 设置关闭预警通知；
  3. 删除保存地点：城市管理页面删除城市 / 乡镇；
  4. 清除缓存：设置页清除天气缓存；
  5. 全部清除本地应用数据：在系统设置中选择“删除 App”。

We follow the principles of legality, necessity, transparency and security to protect your personal information. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the app. By tapping “Agree & Continue”, you acknowledge and accept this policy.

Notes: 1. Accepting this policy means you understand the data required for basic app functions. Non‑essential permissions will be requested separately when you use related features. 2. Permissions are not enabled by default. You may disable permissions in system settings at any time. Denying permissions will not break unrelated features. 3. If you decline this policy, the App stays on the welcome screen, where you can read the full policy. After accepting the policy, you may deny location permission and search for places manually.

1. Information We Collect

Location (Optional): When you first use the location feature, and after you grant permission, we obtain GPS coordinates through iOS Core Location to determine your area and show local weather and alerts. We request location “while using the app” only and do not use background location. After permission is granted, the App may refresh your current location when it comes to the foreground; you can also tap the location button. Coordinates are only sent to weather providers and are not used for advertising. You may deny location and add cities manually.

Notifications (Optional): With your permission, the App generates local reminders for new weather alerts retrieved while the App is in the foreground. When the App is fully terminated, it does not actively fetch weather data and cannot remind you about newly issued alerts.

Local Preferences: City list, temperature unit, notification settings and policy consent status are stored locally on your device and not uploaded to servers.

Senior Briefing and Speech: The briefing setting and identifiers for previously notified alerts are stored on your device to remember your display preference and avoid repeated notifications. Speech uses Apple's system speech synthesis to read weather text. It does not use the microphone or record audio, and no speech text or audio is uploaded to our servers.

We do not retain location history, build user profiles, or use weather query information for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

  • Show real‑time weather, forecasts, air quality, indices and weather alerts for your selected cities;
  • Generate local reminders for new weather alerts retrieved in the foreground;
  • Display the senior briefing and read weather text when you tap the speech button;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We will not use your data for other purposes unless we obtain your separate consent.

3. Sharing & Third‑Party Services

We do not sell your personal information or share it for third‑party advertising. Data may be disclosed only when required by law, court order or necessary to protect safety. To provide the relevant functions, the App uses the following system and weather data services:

  • Apple system services for location, place name resolution, local notifications and speech synthesis: Core Location, CLGeocoder, UserNotifications and AVSpeechSynthesizer
  • Weather data providers receive the queried place name or coordinates and network information needed for the connection, such as the IP address and request timestamp, to provide place search and weather data. The App uses network APIs and does not integrate a third‑party weather SDK

These third parties process data under their own privacy policies. Apple services are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. The weather provider’s data storage and transfer rules are defined by its own privacy policy, we do not make representations on cross‑border transfer.

4. Data Storage & Security

Saved places, preferences, weather cache and notification identifiers are stored locally. Coordinates or place names are still sent to the services above when you make a query. Weather cache is used for about ten minutes to reduce repeated requests and may be shown as historical data after a request fails; cache older than twenty‑four hours is marked expired. Up to 256 notification identifiers are kept and removed after seven days. Clearing weather cache deletes its on‑disk file; removing a saved place deletes that saved entry. To remove all local application data, choose “Delete App” in iPhone settings; “Offload App” may retain documents and data. App data is stored in the iOS sandbox and network requests use HTTPS. If you enable iCloud Backup, Apple manages backups under its own terms.

5. Your Rights

  1. Revoke location permission in iPhone Settings;
  2. Turn off notifications in system settings or inside the app;
  3. Remove cities in city management page;
  4. Clear weather cache in app settings;
  5. Erase local application data by choosing “Delete App” in system settings.

6. Minors

This app is intended for general audiences, not designed for children under 14. Minors under 14 should use it only with guardian review and consent. We will promptly delete minor data collected without guardian approval.

7. Policy Updates

We may revise this policy. The “Last Updated” date will be changed. Material changes will be notified in‑app and we may re‑request consent. Old versions are archived. Your statutory rights will not be reduced without your explicit consent.
